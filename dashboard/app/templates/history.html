TWR and XIRR answer different questions and diverge materially for a monthly contributor. CAGR is time-weighted — how the strategy did, ignoring when money arrived, and the right number against an index. XIRR is money-weighted — what you earned given when your money was at risk. §11n's decision turned on the difference.
